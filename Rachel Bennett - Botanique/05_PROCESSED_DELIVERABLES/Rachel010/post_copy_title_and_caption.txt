Title: Put baby oil on your thinning middle part and watch what happens 😱 (Cuticle Moisture Trick)

Caption:
If you're noticing your middle part getting wider, strands snapping when brushed, or extreme dryness that no expensive mask fixes... you don't need another $40 salon treatment. You need to lock in moisture! 🌿✨

Here is my quick in-salon cuticle seal routine:
1️⃣ Apply a few drops of pure baby/mineral oil along damp parted roots.
2️⃣ Work gently from roots down to ends.
3️⃣ Leave on for 20 minutes before shampooing thoroughly.

The mineral oil creates an instant hydrophobic shield that stops moisture from evaporating and prevents breakage!

⚠️ But remember: If your hair is actively shedding from the root due to years of stress and high cortisol, no topical oil alone can restart dormant follicles.
